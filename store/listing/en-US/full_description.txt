Track internships, hackathons and deadlines that actually fit your profile.

Sagitta is a career workspace for college students — find openings worth your time, save them, apply, and keep the deadlines from sneaking past you. Your WhatsApp groups stay; the deadline panic goes.

VERIFIED CATALOG, A NUMBER YOU CAN CHECK
Every listing carries what you need to decide: stipend, duration, location, mode, skills asked for, and the closing date. New verified listings land every day. Listings we cannot verify are left out rather than padded in — you can test that against any internship you already know about.

DEADLINE AND MATCH ALERTS
Save an opportunity and Sagitta watches it. You get notified when a deadline is close and when something new arrives that fits the skills on your profile. A form closing on Friday will not become a Sunday discovery — save it and check the alert yourself.

SKILL-BASED MATCHING
Your profile is built from the skills you actually have. Match scores are computed against those skills, and a listing with nothing in common with your profile is not shown as a 90% match to make the screen look busy. Edit that list any time and the skill radar redraws from it — you can verify the match score against real listings on day one.

SHARE INTO SAGITTA
When a classmate forwards an opportunity in the group, share it straight into Sagitta. The app searches the verified catalog for what was shared so you can save and track it with one tap — the group finds it, Sagitta keeps it.

APPLICATIONS TRACKER
Every opportunity you apply to moves through stages — applied, interview, offer, rejected — with the date you touched it. Your pipeline is a list you can read, not a pile of browser tabs.

DSA PRACTICE AND GROWTH
Track problems solved across platforms, keep a daily streak, and see a growth score built from what you did in the app: practice, projects, consistency, certifications and goals. Nothing in the score is decorative.

PLACEMENT FORECAST
A readiness reading based on your current profile, with the specific gaps that are holding it down, and how the number has moved over time.

PORTFOLIO AND CAREER MIRROR
Collect projects, certifications and achievements into a portfolio you can share, and see your own history reflected back with the choices that moved you forward.

AI CAREER HELP THAT HAS READ YOUR PROFILE
Ask about a specific role, a resume line, or why you got rejected, and the answer is built from the skills, projects and applications already on your profile rather than a generic listicle. Sagitta also studies a target role into a roadmap, finds the gap between you and a listing, drafts the post for an achievement you just earned, runs a mock interview, and summarises your week.

OFFLINE FIRST
Your saved list, applications, streak and progress live on your device. Open the app on a bus with no signal and it still shows everything you have. When a connection returns, it syncs.

PRIVACY
No ads, no third-party trackers, and your data is never sold. If you sign in and turn on sync, your profile and pipeline are backed up to your own account and to nobody else's. AI questions are relayed through our own server to a model provider and used only to produce your answer — providers are not allowed to train on them. Usage analytics (screens opened, features used) are collected to improve the app. Community contributions are anonymised. Notifications use only the deadlines and saved items already on your device. You can export everything or delete your account from inside the app, and the deletion is real — it removes your data rather than hiding it. Full policy: humoge7502.github.io/Nexus/privacy-policy.html

BUILT FOR THE WAY STUDENTS ACTUALLY LOOK
Shortlist on the metro. Check a deadline between lectures. Log an application the minute you submit it. Everything fits in one hand and works on phones from Android 8 upward.

Sagitta is free to use, with no ads and no sponsored listings — an opportunity appears because it matches you, not because someone paid for the slot. Finding, saving, tracking and practising cost nothing. AI answers are capped at 10 a day on the free tier; an optional Pro upgrade lifts that cap, with a 3-month Season Pass sized to your placement season.

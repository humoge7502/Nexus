Everything buildable/configurable has been done and verified. What follows is the
exact remaining path to the Play Store. Steps 1–4 are account/payment actions that
require the account owner's money and identity; no tooling can substitute for them.

## Step 1 — OpenRouter API key (free, 5 minutes)

1. Create a free account at <https://openrouter.ai> (email + password).
2. Keys → Create Key → copy it.
3. Keep it handy for Step 3. **Never commit it.**

## Step 2 — Firebase Blaze plan (payment method required)

1. <https://console.firebase.google.com/project/nexus-app-6acc7/usage/details>
2. Upgrade to Blaze (Spark → Blaze). A payment method on the account is required.
3. Blaze itself charges only for usage beyond the free tier; the app's AI quota
   (40 calls/day free tier) keeps costs near zero at launch.

## Step 3 — Deploy the AI backend (can be run for you after Step 2)

```bash
cd backend
firebase functions:secrets:set OPENROUTER_API_KEY   # paste the Step-1 key
firebase deploy --only functions
```

## Step 4 — Play Console (USD 25 + identity verification)

1. <https://play.google.com/console> — register (one-time USD 25 fee, identity
   verification on the account owner).
2. Create app "Sagitta" (package `com.nexus.app` — already fixed).
3. Upload `app-release.aab` to internal testing first; test on a real device.
4. Complete the store listing from `store/listing/en-US/`: screenshots (emulator
   captures on the Desktop are a starting point), the launch film as the promo
   video, the privacy policy URL
   (`https://humoge7502.github.io/Nexus/privacy-policy.html`), and the Data Safety
   form (data stored: email, display name, college, career progress — all in
   Firebase; no third-party tracking beyond Firebase Analytics; no ads).
5. Content rating questionnaire, target audience (13+), release to production.

## Regular opportunity updates (already built)

- Feed: `SyncWorker` refreshes `feed/opportunities.json` every six hours on
  unmetered network, plus an explicit pull-to-refresh on the feed screens;
  expired deadlines are hidden even offline.
- Notifications: `DailyBriefingWorker` at 07:00, `ProactiveInsightWorker` at
  08:00, `EveningReviewWorker` at 21:00, `StreakWorker` daily, and
  `DeadlineAlertWorker` sweeping every six hours. The deadline sweep watches
  only items the student saved or is already applying to, and warns at a week
  out (4-7 days), at 2-3 days, the day before, the morning of, and after 18:00
  on the closing day. Each warning fires once per opportunity, and at most one
  notification leaves a sweep, so a heavy pipeline cannot become a stack of
  pings. Deadlines in the feed are calendar days, so no reminder claims a
  precision the source never published.
- Telemetry: `WeeklyRetentionWorker` writes one local retention row a week
  (active days plus only those surfaces holding the student's own data);
  `LeetCodeSyncWorker` every 12 h; `FirestoreSyncWorker` for accounts that sign
  in; `CareerMemoryMaintenanceWorker` for local pruning.
- All schedules live in `core/sync/src/main/kotlin/com/nexus/sync/SyncScheduler.kt`.

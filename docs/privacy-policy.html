Sagitta Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Sagitta is a deadline-first opportunity app for students in India: one verified feed of internships, hackathons, scholarships, fellowships and programmes, with reminders, an application pipeline and AI study help. It is published by an independent developer. This policy explains, in plain language, what data Sagitta handles and why.

The short version

  • We never sell your data, and we never show ads. Sagitta contains no third-party ad SDKs and no third-party trackers.
  • The opportunity feed itself contains no personal data. It is a list of public openings, each with its source and a last-verified timestamp.
  • Your career data (profile, saved opportunities, applications, study progress) is stored in your own Firebase-backed account plus your device.
  • When you use AI features, the text you send is processed by an AI provider only to generate your answer. We do not allow providers to train on your prompts.
  • You can export or permanently delete everything you have given us from inside the app (Settings → Privacy, or Settings → Delete Account).

We do not collect contacts, location, photos, audio, or health data. Notifications use only the deadlines and saved items already on your device.

AI features and third parties

AI requests are relayed by a server function controlled by the developer to an AI model provider (currently OpenRouter-fronted models). Your messages are used only to produce your response. Resume text you submit is used only for that analysis. Opportunity listings themselves are fetched from a static feed of public sources and require an account for nothing.

Permissions

  • Notifications (optional): a daily briefing, streak alerts, and a deadline reminder for each opportunity you saved or have already started applying to — roughly a week out, three days out, the day before, the day it closes, and once again in its final evening. Each of those fires at most once per opportunity. Turn these off any time in Settings.
  • Internet access: required for feed sync and AI features.
  • File access: only when you pick a resume PDF to analyze.

Children

Sagitta is intended for students aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

  • Export: Settings → Export My Data produces a copy of your career data.
  • Delete: Settings → Delete Account permanently removes your profile, saved items, applications, chat history and every other record we hold, in line with Google's account-deletion policy.
  • Notifications: Settings → Notifications.

India's DPDP Act, 2023

Sagitta follows data minimisation: we collect only what a feature needs, we state the purpose at collection, consent for optional features is separable and revocable, and you have a correction and deletion path inside the app. Campus analytics, where offered, are aggregate and consent-based; no individual student is identifiable in a report shared with a college.

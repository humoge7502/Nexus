Ascent Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Ascent is an AI-powered career co-pilot app for Indian CSE students, published by an independent developer. This policy explains, in plain language, what data the app handles and why.

The short version

  • Your career data (profile, applications, study progress) is stored in your own Firebase-backed account and is never sold or shared with advertisers.
  • When you use AI features, the text you send is processed by an AI provider to generate your answer. Prompts are not used to advertise to you.
  • The app contains no third-party ad SDKs.
  • You can export or permanently delete all of your data from inside the app (Settings → Privacy, or Settings → Delete Account).

AI features and third parties

AI requests are relayed by a server function controlled by the developer to an AI model provider (currently OpenRouter-fronted models). Your messages are used only to produce your response. We do not permit providers to train on your prompts. Resume text you submit is used only for that analysis.

Permissions

  • Notifications (optional): daily career briefing, application deadline reminders and streak alerts. You can turn these off any time in Settings.
  • Internet access: required for sync and AI features.
  • File access: only when you pick a resume PDF to analyze.

Children

Ascent is intended for students aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

  • Export: Settings → Export My Data produces a copy of your career data.
  • Delete: Settings → Delete Account permanently removes your profile, applications, chat history and every other record we hold, in line with Google's account-deletion policy.
  • Notifications: Settings → Notifications.
